# Data Analyst Capstone Project: Restaurant Marketing Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview 
* **Goal:** A insurance agency wants to analyze the data to research healthcare premium costs and to analyze the different body types and the environment that affect the premium. The disease's effect or the cost of treatment differs depending on the circumstances.
* **Dataset:** This project heps to create a model that will predict the cost of medical insurance based on various input features across 1300+ rows of insurance data

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
The following tools have been used to analyse the data
* **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn):** Used for Data inspection, cleaning, manipulation and visualization
---

## ⚙️ Project Workflow (How it Was Made)
1. **Data Prep:** Cleaned 1,300+ rows of data, removed duplicates, and handled null values.
2. Converted the data into dataframe using pandas
3. **Exploratory Analysis:** Used Matplotlib and Seaborn bar charts, histograms, boxplots, and heatmaps to identify correlations between various factors such as age, bmi, children and premium charges
4. Added conclusions after each analysis for easier explanation and description and Final observation section for detailed overview of the analysis

---

## 💡 Key Insights (What I Found)
* **Insight 1:** Factors such as Age, BMI and Smoking status have a significant impact on the Insurance premium charges.
* **Insight 2:** Smoking status has the most impact on the Insurance premium charges.
* **Insight 3:** Smokers and non smokers have to pay increasing premium as their age increases but Smokers have a much higher steep as compared to non smokers.
* **Insight 4:** Analysis indicates that bad lifetyle, along with smoking among poeple has a bad impact upon thier health also raising thier premium charges due to higher medical risk and treatment cost.
